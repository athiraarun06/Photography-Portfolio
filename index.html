Photography Terms

Aperture
Controls how much light enters the camera lens.
ISO
Measures the camera sensor's sensitivity to light.
Shutter Speed
Determines how long the camera sensor is exposed to light.

Steps I Follow While Taking Photos

  1. Planning
    1. Choose a good location.
    2. Check the weather conditions.
    3. Carry all camera equipment.
  2. Taking Photos
    1. Adjust camera settings.
    2. Capture photos from different angles.
    3. Select the best shots.
  3. Editing
    1. Adjust brightness and contrast.
    2. Crop unwanted areas.
    3. Save the final image.
